Primality certificates in Lean

  • Mathlib's primality tactic checks the Lucas certificates this page prints.
  • PrimeCert proves primality in Lean 4 with Mathlib from Pocklington certificates, checked by the kernel.
  • hex-primality proves primality in Lean 4 without Mathlib, from certificates checked by the kernel.

Factoring

  • factordb collects known factorizations. The factor hints of the gallery come from it.
  • GMP-ECM implements the elliptic curve method.
  • YAFU factors automatically with ECM, the quadratic sieve and the number field sieve.
  • msieve implements the quadratic sieve and the number field sieve.
  • CADO-NFS implements the number field sieve.
  • Alpertron's integer factorization calculator runs ECM and the quadratic sieve in the browser.

Primality certificates in other proof assistants

  • Coqprime proves primality in Coq from Pocklington and elliptic curve certificates.
  • Pratt's Primality Certificates in Isabelle's Archive of Formal Proofs proves primality from Pratt certificates, using its entry on Lehmer's theorem.
